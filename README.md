# Netflix Strategic Analysis

Business strategy project analysing **Netflix, its competitive environment and its strategic positioning in the streaming industry**.

## Objective

The project examines how Netflix built and maintained its competitive position in a rapidly evolving digital entertainment market.

The analysis covers both the company's external environment and its internal resources.

## Topics covered

- Streaming market analysis
- Competitive environment
- Customer demand
- Suppliers and distribution
- Internal resources and capabilities
- International expansion
- Technology and innovation
- Strategic risks and opportunities

## Strategic frameworks

The project applies several management and strategy tools:

- PESTEL analysis
- Porter's Five Forces
- Value Chain analysis
- SWOT analysis
- TOWS analysis

## Key focus

The study looks at how Netflix developed its business model, adapted to technological change and differentiated itself in an increasingly competitive streaming market.
